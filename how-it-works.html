Quick answers
You get 10 free credits when you sign up — no card needed. Each stamp costs 1 credit for text, 2 for URLs, 3 for files. Bitcoin archive is 5 additional credits. Top up anytime from the dashboard.
Bitcoin archive is an optional extra layer where we embed your TSA-1 proof manifest directly into the Bitcoin blockchain via OP_RETURN. It's the strongest possible permanence — your proof becomes part of Bitcoin's immutable history, independently verifiable by anyone using any block explorer, completely without TruthStamp. Most users don't need it — MegaETH + Arweave is already extremely permanent. But for proofs where you want the absolute maximum, Bitcoin archive adds that final layer.
Nothing changes for your proofs. They live on MegaETH and Arweave — public, decentralized networks that exist independently of us. Your receipts are verifiable forever using open tools, even without our website. Bitcoin-archived proofs add yet another independent layer.
Cryptographic timestamps are increasingly recognized in legal disputes as strong evidence of prior existence. We're not a law firm and can't give legal advice — but a verifiable on-chain timestamp is significantly stronger than a screenshot or cloud document.
You can hide a stamp from public view, but the on-chain record is permanent and cannot be deleted. That's the whole point — it's tamper-proof. Once sealed, always sealed.
